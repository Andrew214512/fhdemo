Services we use

Supabase (database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Google and Apple (if you choose to sign in with them), Open Library and Google Books (book information, no personal data sent), and [EasyPost] (package tracking: tracking numbers only). [Email provider] sends sign-in and exchange emails.

Photos

Profile photos are resized in your browser before upload, which removes the hidden location data many phones add to pictures.

We don't sell your data

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). We remove your profile, photo, address, wishlist and alerts, and your sign-in. We keep a record of past exchanges and payments, without your name, for accounting and legal reasons, for [7 years].
