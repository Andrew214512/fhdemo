3. How exchanges work

  • You can list physical books you own. You must describe each book's condition honestly.
  • When another reader requests your book and you accept, you agree to ship it within 3 days, at your own cost, and add tracking.
  • When a book you sent is delivered and confirmed (or 2 days pass without a reported problem), you receive 1 book credit.
  • You can spend 1 credit to request an available book from any reader. Your credit is reserved when you request and spent when the book is confirmed.
  • If a request isn't accepted, isn't shipped in time, or is lost in the mail, your reserved credit is returned.

4. Book credits

Credits are a record of books you've exchanged. They have no cash value, can't be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money, and don't expire while your account is open. Wormy may correct credits created by error or abuse. Credits end when your account is closed.
